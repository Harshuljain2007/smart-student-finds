College Essentials: A Useful Everyday Checklist

A practical student guide. Consider your needs and budget before buying.

Before buying: Check compatibility, dimensions, return policy, warranty and current price. Not every student needs every item.

1. A comfortable bag

Choose a bag that fits your daily load and has comfortable straps.

2. Water bottle

Consider capacity, leak resistance and ease of cleaning.

3. Notebook and stationery

Carry what your classes require and check instructor preferences.

4. Charger and cable

Use compatible chargers and avoid damaged cables.

5. Earphones or headset

Useful for calls and lectures where permitted; keep volume safe.

6. Student ID

Keep your ID and essential documents in a consistent, secure place.

7. Small personal essentials

Pack only what you need for your schedule.

How to choose thoughtfully

  • Identify the problem you want to solve.
  • Compare specifications, recent reviews and return terms.
  • Start with one useful item instead of buying everything.
