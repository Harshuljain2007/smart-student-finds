7 Laptop Accessories Students Should Consider

A practical student guide. Consider your needs and budget before buying.

Before buying: Check compatibility, dimensions, return policy, warranty and current price. Not every student needs every item.

1. Laptop stand

A stable stand can raise the screen closer to eye level. Check height, stability, portability and airflow.

2. Mouse

Choose a comfortable shape and a connection type that works with your laptop.

3. Keyboard

Compare layout, typing feel, size and wired or wireless connection.

4. USB hub or adapter

Check your laptop's exact ports. USB-C does not guarantee every hub feature will work.

5. Headphones

Consider comfort, microphone quality and a suitable volume level.

6. Laptop sleeve

Measure your laptop and compare the sleeve's internal dimensions and padding.

7. Cable organiser

A small pouch or reusable cable ties can keep chargers from tangling.

How to choose thoughtfully

  • Identify the problem you want to solve.
  • Compare specifications, recent reviews and return terms.
  • Start with one useful item instead of buying everything.
